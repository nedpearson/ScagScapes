Multiples below are medians from recorded transactions, not opinion — BizBuySell (945 landscaping sales) and the IBBA Market Pulse (203 closed deals, Q1 2026).

This page mirrors the growth blueprint's model: the operating plan, the capital that pays for it, and what the company is worth at the end. The growth pace is simplified into one slider instead of the workbook's year-by-year gates, so the two will not agree to the dollar. Most years they land within a few percent; in a year where the crew count falls on the other side of a hiring threshold they can differ by 10–15%, because an admin, an operations manager and an estimator each get hired at a whole-person step rather than gradually. Those steps are real — you cannot hire two thirds of an estimator — which is also why operating profit dips in the year a crew is added. The workbook is the authoritative version; this page is for exploring, not for quoting.

On the multiples. They are not one number, because the multiple depends on who is allowed to buy you. Under $2M of revenue the buyer is an individual using an SBA loan and the price is set by what his debt service can carry — a narrow 2.0–2.3× band on owner's cash no matter how good the business is. Over $2M the buyer pool becomes searchers and regional platforms at around 4× EBITDA. Over $5M it becomes private equity at 5–8×. Medians are from BizBuySell's closed sales (945 landscaping businesses, 2021–2025) and the IBBA Market Pulse (203 closed transactions, Q1 2026). Worth knowing: a recurring service book does not lift the earnings multiple at this size — pest control, the most contract-recurring home service there is, sells at 2.04× owner's cash, below landscaping's 2.30×. Growing is what moves the multiple.

On the equity comparison. Both paths are priced on the identical dollars, so the difference is the real cost of selling a share rather than borrowing. Nothing here is a valuation, an offer, or investment advice — it is arithmetic on assumptions you can change, and a CPA and an attorney should price and paper anything anyone actually signs. Every number is computed live in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere, and this page does not connect to the real Scag Scapes data.
